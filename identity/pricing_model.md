# Kaide Labs — Pricing Model

## Two-component pricing

**£10,000/month base** — standing capacity, monitoring, maintenance, minor adjustments for one deployed sidecar. 50% upfront, fully refundable in month 1 (clause removed after 2-3 customers signed).

**£15,000-£25,000 per net-new sidecar sprint** — one-time, charged on delivery, per new sidecar architected and shipped (48-72hr build).

## Volume step-ups

- 3+ sidecars per customer: base steps to £13-15k/month
- 5+ sidecars per customer: base steps to £18-20k/month

Reflects operational load of maintaining multiple sidecars in parallel.

## Refund discipline

First-month refundable clause is the trust accelerator for cold prospects. Comes off after 2-3 customers have signed and the engagement model has logo proof.

## Investor-facing framing

Base + per-sprint reads as services-revenue + product-revenue on the books. Fundable shape. Defends against agency frame: agencies charge for hours; Kaide Labs charges for capacity (base) plus delivered artifacts (per-sprint). Two distinct economic logics, neither of which is "bodies for hire."

## Capital deployment discipline

Do not deploy customer payment to infrastructure or hires until:
1. Both 50% halves cleared
2. Month-1 refund window closed without invocation

Treat received-but-not-cleared revenue as in-escrow. Build the habit early, when stakes are small.
