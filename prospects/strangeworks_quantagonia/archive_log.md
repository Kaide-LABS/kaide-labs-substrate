# Strangeworks / Quantagonia — Archive Log

## What they do

Quantum computing optimization tooling for enterprise clients.

## Why considered

Two full PRD cycles surfaced architecture options. The OpenClaw approach was identified as a strategic community-relationship hook targeting Philipp Hannemann's quantum community.

## Why archived

Quantum AI is too niche for Kaide Labs' current motion. The total addressable prospect pool is small, the procurement bottleneck shape doesn't match the FDE wedge cleanly, and the customer base skews toward research/academic which has long cycles and low recurring demand.

## Generalizable learnings

- Strategic community-relationship hooks are an interesting outreach mechanism but require dedicated relationship investment that doesn't scale across prospects.
- Niche verticals (small TAM) are not where Kaide Labs' substrate compounds best. Prefer verticals with 20+ viable candidates in the seed-to-Series-A range.
