# TORTUS AI — Archive Log

## What they do

Clinical AI for doctors — ambient conversation capture, EHR write-back, lab requests, coding. NHS DTAC compliant. Multiple NHS deployments + Great Ormond Street research partnership.

## Why considered

Surfaced during May 2026 sourcing pass. Strong window-fit on paper: Khosla Ventures seed Feb 2024 (~27 months ago at outer edge of sweet spot), 15 employees, NHS multi-site deployment, expansion into UK/Canada/US (three compliance jurisdictions).

## Why archived

TORTUS is too deep in their clinical vertical to engage with a generalist FDE strike team. Their procurement-grade work (DTAC compliance, EHR-specific integrations, clinical safety cases) requires vendors who themselves have deep clinical specialization. They would only engage with equally-deep clinical specialists.

This is a structural mismatch: Kaide Labs' wedge is horizontal-integration-bottleneck-absorption across multiple verticals. Deep-clinical-specialist prospects need vertical specialists, not horizontal generalists.

## Revisit conditions

Revisitable IF Kaide Labs eventually builds dedicated clinical-AI specialization OR if TORTUS expands into less-clinical-specialist procurement work (general InfoSec questionnaires, customer-facing documentation) where horizontal expertise applies.

## Generalizable learnings

### Deep vertical specialists require matching vertical depth

When a prospect's procurement work is itself deeply vertical-specialized (clinical safety cases, financial compliance attestations, etc.), they will only engage with vendors who match that specialization. Kaide Labs' horizontal motion fits prospects whose procurement work is *generalist enterprise* (InfoSec questionnaires, integration scoping, deployment scaffolding) — not prospects whose procurement work is itself vertical-deep.

Apply this filter during Step 0 fit assessment: read the company's case studies and customer-facing materials. If the procurement work they describe is itself dense with vertical-specific terminology and process, archive — don't pursue.
