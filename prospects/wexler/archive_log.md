# Wexler AI — Archive Log

## What they do

Legal AI for litigation / e-discovery. UK-based.

## Why considered

Three architectures were red-teamed (Gemini-generated). The Pre-Submission Bundle Attestation Sidecar emerged as the winning architecture, anchored to verified founder statements.

## Why archived

Pipeline priority shifted to industrial-AI prospects (Matta, Solvo) that were structurally insulated from frontier-lab commoditization pressure. Legal AI was placed lower priority because the vertical was about to feel acute commoditization pressure from Claude Cowork's 20+ legal connector launch (which subsequently materialized in May 2026).

## Revisit conditions

Not revisitable in current form. The legal AI vertical is now under acute structural pressure. Future legal-AI prospects worth evaluating only if they have deep IP moat (specific corpus, neuro-symbolic stack, vertical depth) that insulates from Cowork commoditization.

## Generalizable learnings

- Architecture generated does not obligate sprint commit. Walking away after Step 1B if priority shifts is operationally correct discipline.
- Legal AI vertical is currently structurally fragile. Apply extra caution at sourcing.
